The future of electric vehicles is accelerating faster than ever, transitioning into a true mass-market era. Global EV sales are projected to hit up to 30 million units, with total fleets surpassing 116 million. China continues its industrial dominance, driving over 60% of worldwide sales while domestic adoption clears 50%. Simultaneously, soaring battery cost reductions below the $100/kWh threshold and ultra-fast 600kW charging corridors are closing the price gap with traditional cars and eradicating range anxiety. Alongside a resurgence in plug-in hybrids, the electric revolution is reshaping global transportation into an affordable, highly efficient reality.
